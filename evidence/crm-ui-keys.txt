Acceptance evidence
Acceptance records your review of customer acceptance, links or creates a customer, marks the opportunity won, and creates an internal sales-ready handoff. It sends no quote, email or message; reserves no stock; and creates no sale, invoice, tax posting or general-ledger entry. All amounts are net estimates; tax is not calculated here. Review currency, tax, stock and pricing separately before posting Sales.
